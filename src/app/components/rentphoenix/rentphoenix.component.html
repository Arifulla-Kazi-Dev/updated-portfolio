Flagship Venture

RentPhoenix

A rental management product being built to make recurring operations more organized, visible, and dependable for landlords and tenants.

The problem space

Rent tracking, scattered records, follow-ups, and coordination should not depend on confusion.

Rent tracking

Structure recurring rent activity with a clearer view of payments and follow-ups.

Landlord-tenant sync

Support more consistent communication and shared visibility around rental workflows.

Digital records

Keep important rental information organized and easier to reference over time.

Automation

Reduce repetitive coordination through purposeful reminders and operational workflows.

Transparency

Design for clarity, accountability, and trust across the rental experience.
