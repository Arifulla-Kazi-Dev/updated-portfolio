About

Learning by building. Leading through execution.

I am Arifulla Kazi, a builder with an Electronics and Communication Engineering background and a practical belief: meaningful products are shaped by staying close to real problems.

My path moved from networks and electronics into software, product thinking, and SaaS. Each project strengthened the same habit: understand the system, simplify the experience, and keep shipping until the idea becomes useful.

That habit became RentPhoenix Technologies. Our first product, RentPhoenix OS, is live and helping landlords and tenants coordinate rent, records, and maintenance. Our second, CO-Founder, brings the same clarity to startup finances. I am growing the company with resilience, curiosity, and disciplined execution.
